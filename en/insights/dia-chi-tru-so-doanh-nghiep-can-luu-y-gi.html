Registered Office Address: What to Check Before Filing

Updated 08/17/20265 min readPrepared by BizLegal

Key legal and operational checks for a company’s registered office in Vietnam.

In this article

01The address must be identifiable02Document the right to use the premises03Check residential and future-change issues

An address is more than a line on the enterprise certificate. It affects registration, tax administration, licensing and official correspondence.

The address must be identifiable

Use a complete address that can be located within the administrative system. Confirm current ward and city information and avoid ambiguous floor or unit details.

Document the right to use the premises

Keep the lease, ownership evidence or other lawful-use documents. Regulated activities may require additional conditions relating to area, safety or facilities.

Check residential and future-change issues

Apartments designated solely for residential use are generally not appropriate as company offices. A later relocation may require updates with registration, tax, banking, licensing and counterparties.

Key point

Verify both the legal status and practical suitability of the premises before using the address in an application.
