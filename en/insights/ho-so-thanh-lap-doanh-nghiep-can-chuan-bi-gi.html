What Documents Are Needed to Establish a Company in Vietnam?

Updated 08/21/20266 min readPrepared by BizLegal

The information and documents founders should align before preparing an enterprise registration application.

In this article

01Company information02Owner and shareholder documents03Registration documents and final review

A complete application starts with consistent information. Names, addresses, business lines, capital and ownership details should match across every document.

Company information

Prepare the proposed Vietnamese name, head-office address, business lines, charter capital and details of the legal representative. The address and regulated activities should be checked before filing.

Owner and shareholder documents

Identity or legal-status documents are required for individual and organizational owners. Foreign-issued documents may require consular legalization and Vietnamese translation, depending on the case.

Registration documents and final review

The application normally includes the registration form, charter and lists or authorization documents relevant to the selected structure.

  • Use the same spelling and identification details throughout
  • Confirm signing authority
  • Check document validity and required attachments
  • Retain a complete signed filing set
Key point

Build one verified information sheet first, then generate every application document from that source.
