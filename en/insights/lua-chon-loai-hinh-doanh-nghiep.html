Should You Establish a Limited Liability Company or a Joint Stock Company?

Updated 08/23/20266 min readPrepared by BizLegal

A practical comparison of ownership, capital raising and governance before choosing a company structure in Vietnam.

In this article

01When an LLC is a practical choice02When a joint stock company fits better03Decide before filing

The right structure should reflect how the founders intend to own, finance and manage the business—not simply which registration file looks shorter.

When an LLC is a practical choice

A limited liability company is often suitable for a closely held business with a defined group of owners. Transfers can be more controlled, and the governance structure is generally easier to operate.

  • One owner or a limited group of members
  • Closer control over ownership changes
  • Simpler internal decision-making

When a joint stock company fits better

A joint stock company is designed for a business that expects a broader shareholder base or future capital raising. That flexibility comes with more formal governance and record-keeping.

  • At least three founding shareholders
  • Share-based capital structure
  • More suitable for multiple investment rounds

Decide before filing

Confirm the intended owners, transfer restrictions, fundraising plan and decision-making model. Changing structure later is possible, but it can affect contracts, tax records and internal approvals.

Key point

Choose the structure around the next stage of the business, not only the day of incorporation.
