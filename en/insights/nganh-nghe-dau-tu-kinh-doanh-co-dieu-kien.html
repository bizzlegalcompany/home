Conditional Business Lines: What Must a Company Verify?

Updated 08/13/20266 min readPrepared by BizLegal

How to distinguish enterprise registration from the licences and operating conditions required for regulated activities.

In this article

01Check three layers02Registration is not a substitute for a licence03Prepare evidence and maintain compliance

Having a business line recorded does not automatically authorize a company to conduct every regulated activity within it.

Check three layers

Review market-access rules, sector licences and ongoing operating conditions. Requirements may relate to capital, personnel, premises, equipment or professional certificates.

Registration is not a substitute for a licence

Enterprise registration records the intended activity. Where specialist law requires approval, the company should obtain it and satisfy the conditions before commencing that activity.

Prepare evidence and maintain compliance

Create a document list for each condition and identify who maintains it after licensing. Some conditions must remain satisfied throughout operation, not only on the filing date.

Key point

Map every intended revenue-generating activity to its licence and compliance requirements before launch.
