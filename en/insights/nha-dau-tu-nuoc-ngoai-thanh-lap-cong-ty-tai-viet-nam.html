Foreign Investors Establishing a Company in Vietnam: A Practical Roadmap

Updated 08/11/20268 min readPrepared by BizLegal

The main review and registration stages from market access to investment and enterprise registration.

In this article

01Review market access and project conditions02Prepare investment registration03Register the enterprise and activate operations

A foreign-invested project should be structured around the investor, activity, location, capital and implementation schedule before documents are drafted.

Review market access and project conditions

Confirm whether the proposed activity is open, conditional or restricted for foreign investors. Ownership limits, partner requirements and sector approvals may affect the structure.

Prepare investment registration

Depending on the project, an investment policy approval may be required before or alongside investment registration. Financial capacity, premises and implementation assumptions should be supported by documents.

Register the enterprise and activate operations

After the relevant investment step, complete enterprise registration and post-licensing tasks such as capital accounts, capital contribution, tax, accounting and specialist licences.

  • Align investor names across foreign and Vietnamese documents
  • Allow time for legalization and translation
  • Keep project milestones realistic
  • Coordinate investment and enterprise records
Key point

Resolve market access and project structure first; document preparation should follow those decisions.
