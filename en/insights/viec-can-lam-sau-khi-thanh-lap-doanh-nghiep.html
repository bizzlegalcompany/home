Post-licensing compliance

What a Company Should Do After Incorporation

Updated 08/15/20267 min readPrepared by BizLegal

A structured post-incorporation checklist covering capital, tax, accounting, invoices, employment and operating licences.

In this article

01Complete corporate and capital records02Set up tax, accounting and transactions03Review people, data and sector licences

Receiving an enterprise registration certificate is the start of the operating phase. The next tasks should be assigned and tracked from day one.

Complete corporate and capital records

Issue internal appointment and governance documents, establish statutory records and monitor capital contributions with suitable payment evidence.

Set up tax, accounting and transactions

Confirm the accounting framework, electronic tax access, digital signature, invoice process and bank account workflow before routine transactions begin.

  • Assign accounting responsibility
  • Define invoice approval controls
  • Keep contracts and supporting documents
  • Monitor filing and payment deadlines

Review people, data and sector licences

Employment, social insurance, personal-data processing and sector-specific conditions may create separate obligations. These should be mapped to the actual operating model.

Key point

Use a dated compliance checklist with a named owner for each post-incorporation task.
